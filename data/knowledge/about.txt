## Early Life and Background

Kuldeep Kumar Mishra was born and raised in the Mithilanchal region of Bihar, specifically in Darbhanga district. He comes from a humble agricultural family with strong values centered around education, hard work, discipline, and continuous learning. His father, Ramesh Mishra, is primarily engaged in farming and also manages a small general store that supports the family's livelihood. Kuldeep belongs to a joint family with deep roots in the Mithila region and grew up in an environment that emphasized perseverance, responsibility, and academic excellence.

His grandfather, Late Pitambar Mishra, was a respected elder of the family whose values and legacy continue to inspire future generations. Kuldeep comes from a family of eight members, including his parents, four elder sisters, a younger brother, and himself.

## School Education

Kuldeep began his educational journey at Rajkiya Madhya Vidyalaya, Asi, Darbhanga, where he completed his primary and middle school education from Class 1 through Class 8. During these formative years, he developed a strong interest in mathematics and analytical thinking.

After completing middle school, he continued his education at +2 High School, Asi, Darbhanga. During his secondary and higher secondary education, his interest in mathematics became increasingly strong, motivating him to pursue higher studies in the subject.

## Undergraduate Education

Following his school education, Kuldeep enrolled in the Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) Mathematics Honours program at Lalit Narayan Mithila University (LNMU), Darbhanga, where he studied from 2019 to 2022. During his undergraduate years, he built a strong foundation in mathematical disciplines including calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, probability, statistics, and mathematical reasoning.

Alongside his university studies, he received additional academic guidance from Pankaj Kumar B.Sc. Mathematics Coaching, where he further strengthened his mathematical problem-solving abilities and competitive examination preparation.

To support himself and gain teaching experience, Kuldeep also worked as a home tutor during his B.Sc. studies. He taught Mathematics to students of Classes 11 and 12, helping them prepare for board examinations and competitive tests. This experience improved his communication skills, teaching abilities, leadership qualities, and conceptual understanding of mathematics.

## Academic Achievement

Kuldeep's dedication to mathematics and analytical thinking culminated in a major academic achievement when he secured All India Rank (AIR) 1180 in IIT JAM Mathematics 2024 among approximately 15,000 candidates across India. This accomplishment earned him admission to the prestigious Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) Lucknow.

## Postgraduate Education

In August 2024, Kuldeep began his Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Data Science at the Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) Lucknow. The program concluded in July 2026. During his postgraduate studies, he transitioned from pure mathematics into advanced fields including Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Agentic AI, Data Engineering, Cloud Computing, and Intelligent Systems.

The rigorous mathematical foundation developed during his undergraduate years became a key strength in understanding and implementing advanced AI algorithms, optimization techniques, machine learning models, and intelligent systems.

## Personal Journey

Kuldeep's journey reflects a progression from a village in the Mithilanchal region of Bihar to becoming an AI Engineer working on advanced technologies such as Agentic AI, Large Language Models, Multi-Agent Systems, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Computer Vision, and Intelligent Automation. His story demonstrates how strong fundamentals in mathematics, consistent self-learning, teaching experience, and dedication to technology can create opportunities for growth and innovation.
